Sell a House That Needs Repairs, As-Is, Anywhere in NC

Problems we buy every week

Foundation and crawlspace issues, roof leaks, water and mold damage, fire damage, hoarder houses, failed septic, outdated electrical and plumbing, code violations and unpermitted additions.

How we price a house that needs work

We estimate what the house will be worth once it's fixed, subtract the repair costs and our costs to buy, hold and resell it, and make our offer from there. We'll walk you through the numbers so you can see how we got to our price.

Compare before you decide

If your house only needs light cosmetic work, listing with an agent may net you more, and we'll tell you so. Our offer makes the most sense when the repairs are large, uncertain or you simply don't want to manage them.
