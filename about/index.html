About Acorn House Buyer

A North Carolina home buying company built on straight answers and fair offers.

Who we are

Acorn House Buyer is a real estate investment company based in the Charlotte area. We buy houses directly from homeowners across North Carolina, mostly homes that need work or owners who need a fast, certain sale.

How we work

Like an acorn that grows into something strong, we believe good deals start small and honest: a real conversation, a fair number with the math shown, and a closing handled by a licensed North Carolina attorney. We don't use pressure tactics, and we're happy to tell you when listing with an agent would serve you better.

What we are not

We are not real estate agents and we don't list houses. We are buyers. When we make you an offer, we either buy the property ourselves or, in some cases, assign the contract to a partner investor, which we will disclose to you. Either way, your price and terms don't change, and you never pay us anything.

Our promise

  • Fair, written offers with the reasoning explained
  • No fees, no commissions, no surprise deductions
  • Every sale closed through a licensed NC closing attorney
  • Your information stays private and is never sold
