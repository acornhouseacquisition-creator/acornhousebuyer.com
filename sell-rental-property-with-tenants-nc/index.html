Tenants can stay

You don't need to empty the property first. We buy with current leases in place and take over as the landlord at closing, including security deposits, which are credited at closing.

Late rent or a pending eviction

If a tenant has stopped paying or you are in the middle of an eviction, we can still make an offer. We will ask what stage the case is in and factor it into the price, so you don't have to see it through.

Deferred maintenance is fine

Worn floors, old roofs, outdated kitchens and code issues are all common in rentals. We price the repairs into our offer, so you don't pay for any of them.
