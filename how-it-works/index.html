How Selling Your House to Us Works

A simple, transparent process with a licensed North Carolina closing attorney handling the money and paperwork.

1. Tell us about your property

Call (704) 246-3399 or send the short form. We'll ask about the house, its condition and your timeline. There's no cost and no pressure.

2. We see the house once

We schedule one walkthrough at a time that suits you, usually within a day or two. If you're out of town, a family member, tenant or lockbox works too, and some homes we can price from photos and video.

3. You get a written cash offer

Usually within 24 hours. We explain how we arrived at the number, including the repair estimate. The offer is all cash, so there's no lender approval or appraisal to wait for.

4. Sign the agreement and pick a date

If you accept, we sign a simple purchase agreement and send it to a North Carolina closing attorney. They search the title and order a payoff from your lender. You decide the closing date.

5. Close and get paid

You sign at the attorney's office or remotely with a mobile notary. The attorney pays off your mortgage and liens and sends your proceeds by wire or check. Leave anything you don't want; we take care of it.

Frequently asked questions

How do you decide what to offer for my house?

We look at recent sales of similar homes nearby to estimate what the house would sell for once it's fully fixed up. Then we subtract the cost of repairs, our closing and holding costs, and a fair profit margin. We show you how we got to the number, and there's no obligation to accept.

Do I pay any fees or commissions?

No. There are no agent commissions and no fees to work with us. We also pay the normal seller-side closing costs. If you have a mortgage or liens, those are paid off from the sale proceeds at closing, just like in any sale.

How fast can you close?

Usually 7 to 14 days after you accept our offer, depending on how quickly the closing attorney can confirm a clear title. If you need more time, you pick the date.

Do I need to make repairs or clean the house?

No. We buy houses as-is, in any condition. You can take what you want and leave the rest behind.

Who handles the closing?

Every sale closes with a licensed North Carolina real estate closing attorney. They check the title, pay off your loans and liens, and send your money by wire or check.
