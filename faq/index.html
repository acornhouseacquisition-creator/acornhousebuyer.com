Selling your house to Acorn House Buyer

How do you decide what to offer for my house?

We look at recent sales of similar homes nearby to estimate what the house would sell for once it's fully fixed up. Then we subtract the cost of repairs, our closing and holding costs, and a fair profit margin. We show you how we got to the number, and there's no obligation to accept.

Do I pay any fees or commissions?

No. There are no agent commissions and no fees to work with us. We also pay the normal seller-side closing costs. If you have a mortgage or liens, those are paid off from the sale proceeds at closing, just like in any sale.

How fast can you close?

Usually 7 to 14 days after you accept our offer, depending on how quickly the closing attorney can confirm a clear title. If you need more time, you pick the date.

Do I need to make repairs or clean the house?

No. We buy houses as-is, in any condition. You can take what you want and leave the rest behind.

Who handles the closing?

Every sale closes with a licensed North Carolina real estate closing attorney. They check the title, pay off your loans and liens, and send your money by wire or check.

Are you real estate agents?

No. Acorn House Buyer is a real estate investment company, not a brokerage. We buy property directly from owners. In some cases, instead of closing ourselves, we may assign our purchase contract to a partner investor. If so, we'll tell you up front, and your price and terms stay the same.

Can I sell if I owe more than the house is worth or have liens?

Often, yes. We can work with the closing attorney on tax liens, judgments and code violations. If you owe more than the home is worth, we can talk about a short sale with your lender.

Is there any obligation if I request an offer?

None. Ask for an offer, review it, and decide. If it doesn't work for you, we'll part as friends and you'll know your options.
